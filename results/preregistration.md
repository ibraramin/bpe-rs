## Metric

- Criterion group `encode_realistic`, arms `serial` (`encode`), `parallel`
  (`encode_parallel`) and `simd` (`encode_simd`, only while the feature exists).
- Primary statistic: Criterion **median** wall-clock time with its **95% CI**
  (`median.estimate`, `median.lower_bound`, `median.upper_bound` in
  `target/criterion/encode_realistic/<arm>/new/estimates.json`). Throughput
  (MiB/s) is recorded but is derived from the same median and is not a separate
  gate.
- Speedup for arm A over baseline B is `median(B) / median(A)`. The conservative
  95% lower bound of that speedup is `lower_bound(B) / upper_bound(A)`. This
  formula is fixed now and applied identically to every run. Raw Criterion JSON
  is copied under `results/criterion-run1/` and `results/criterion-run2/`.
- Repetitions: **2 independent full `cargo bench --all-features` runs**. A claim
  must hold in **both** runs.

## Parallel decision rule (fixed before measurement)

- **KEEP** the `parallel` feature only if, in **both** runs:
  1. `speedup(parallel over serial) >= 1.25`, and
  2. its conservative 95% lower bound `> 1.0`, and
  3. `cargo test --all-features` passes with byte-identical ids.
- **REVERT** the `parallel` feature (feature, method and arms) if any gate is
  missed in either run. This is ADR-001's recorded change-mind clause: "benchmark
  shows no reproducible win, then revert." A documented no-win is **not** an
  acceptable substitute for reverting. The negative result, with the real
  numbers, stays in `results/benchmark-results.md` either way.

## SIMD decision rule (fixed before measurement)

- The candidate is `encode_simd` behind feature `simd`, `std::arch` x86_64 only,
  no new dependency.
- **KEEP** only if, in **both** runs:
  1. `speedup(simd over the parallel scalar path) >= 1.5`, and
  2. its conservative 95% lower bound `> 1.0`, and
  3. all tests pass.
- `speedup(simd over serial)` is also reported for every run so a win cannot be
  an artifact of choosing a slow baseline; it is informational, not a substitute
  for the mandatory comparison against `parallel`.
- Otherwise **REMOVE** the `simd` feature, method, tests and bench arm, and record
  the negative result with the actual measured numbers.

## Falsification precedent (live risk)

The Phase 4 experiment measured AVX2 matmul *slower* than scalar at
128x128x128. The same outcome is expected here: thread setup and SSE2's lack of
integer gather can erase the apparent advantage. No win may be claimed from a
post-hoc metric, a subset of runs, or a different corpus. Never invent numbers.
